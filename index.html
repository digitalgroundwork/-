§ 06 — Questions

Before you ask, we probably already answered it.

Do I own the website?
Yes. The domain, content, and design are yours. If you ever leave, we hand everything over — no lock-in, no holding your site hostage.
How long does a build actually take?
A one-page site can go from blueprint call to live in under 24 hours. A full multi-page site is usually 1–2 weeks depending on how much content and back-and-forth is needed.
Do I need to write the content myself?
No — we write copy that's written for your customers, based on what you tell us on the blueprint call. You review and edit anything before it goes live.
What happens if I want to make a change later?
Send us the change and we make it — usually the same week. Your plan includes a set number of edits per month; the Growth and E-Commerce+ plans also give you basic self-editing access.
What if I want to cancel?
There's no contract — cancel the monthly plan any time. If you cancel, hosting stops, so most clients move the site to their own hosting first; we'll help with that handoff.
Do you only build for certain types of businesses?
Mostly local service businesses and small shops — plumbers, dentists, contractors, boutiques, and similar. If you need a large marketplace, a complex web app, or something with heavy custom software behind it, we're probably not the right fit — happy to point you in a better direction.
