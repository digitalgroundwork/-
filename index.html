What's Not Included?

A custom domain name (for example, yourbusiness.com) is not included in the package price. Domain registration and renewal are separate costs paid by the client. Hosting, paid third-party services, premium plugins, external platform subscriptions, and ongoing maintenance are also excluded unless explicitly stated in your project proposal.

A free hosting platform or a platform-provided subdomain is not the same as owning a custom domain.
